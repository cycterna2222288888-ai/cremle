Ekaterina
Mizulina

The internet as a field of repression. Censorship as the family business.

Head of the Safe Internet League
Elena Mizulina — senator, author of the anti-LGBT law
Resource blocking, mass complaints, platform pressure
YouTube, opposition Telegram channels, independent media
Not under personal sanctions
Safe Internet League · Internet Censorship
01

Biography

Ekaterina Mizulina — director of the League of Safe Internet (LBI), Russia's primary state-linked censorship infrastructure. Daughter of Senator Elena Mizulina, architect of the "LGBT propaganda" law. Where her mother legislates restrictions, Ekaterina implements them: blocking, flagging, and reporting content across Russian social media. EU sanctions 2024.

1985

Born into a political family

Born 1985, daughter of Elena Mizulina — State Duma deputy, later Federation Council senator, author of the law banning "LGBT propaganda to minors" (2013, extended 2023). The family surname has become synonymous with conservative internet censorship in Russia. Fact

2011

Founds the League of Safe Internet

Creates the League of Safe Internet (Лига Безопасного Интернета, LBI) with initial funding from state-connected telecommunications companies. Official mandate: protect children from harmful online content. Actual function: a rapid-response censorship infrastructure that flags political content alongside genuinely harmful material. Interp.

2013–2019

Expansion of blocking infrastructure

LBI works with Roskomnadzor to build Russia's internet filtering system. Coordinates with courts to fast-track blocking orders. By 2019 processes thousands of reports per month. Develops "crowdsourced reporting": LBI channels and bots direct followers to report opposition content to authorities. Fact

2020–2021

COVID and Navalny — two tests

During the pandemic runs campaigns against "COVID disinformation" that in practice target independent health commentary. After Navalny's poisoning and return to Russia, LBI Telegram channels systematically report Navalny-related content to Roskomnadzor. Multiple platforms receive blocking orders during this period. Interp.

2022

Wartime censorship acceleration

After the invasion LBI becomes a front-line censorship actor. Its Telegram channel (500,000+ subscribers) distributes instructions for reporting anti-war content, advocates blocking Instagram and Facebook (both blocked in Russia March 2022), and coordinates with Roskomnadzor on "discrediting the army" cases under new wartime censorship laws. Fact

2024

EU sanctions — 14th package

Designated under the EU's 14th sanctions package (June 2024, Council Decision 2024/1510). Ground: "responsible for actions undermining the freedom of the press and media in Russia." Asset freeze, EU travel ban. First LBI leadership member to be sanctioned. Fact

03

Method

Mizulina's model is "crowdsourced censorship": LBI does not block content directly — it mobilises a network of followers and automated tools to report content to Roskomnadzor at scale, ensuring that political speech is treated identically to genuinely harmful material (child abuse, drug sales) in Russia's blocking system. By embedding political censorship within a child-safety framework she insulates the operation from obvious criticism. Interp.

The LBI Telegram channel functions as an operations centre: it identifies target content, provides step-by-step reporting instructions, and announces "victories" (successful blocks) to maintain follower motivation. During the 2022 invasion it pivoted from children's safety to wartime information control within days — demonstrating that the "child protection" mandate was always a cover for a broader political function. Interp.

The dynastic dimension is functional, not merely symbolic. Elena Mizulina (mother) provides legislative architecture; Ekaterina (daughter) provides operational implementation. The mother creates the laws, the daughter enforces them through civil-society mechanisms. This division of labour makes it harder to challenge either: legislators claim they only write laws, civil-society actors claim they only report violations of existing law. Interp.

04

Sanctions

EU — personal sanctions, 14th package, June 2024 (Council Decision 2024/1510). Ground: "responsible for actions undermining the freedom of the press and media in Russia; director of the League of Safe Internet, which is actively involved in censoring Russian media and social networks, including wartime censorship." Asset freeze, travel ban to all 27 EU member states. Fact

Mizulina's designation is the most recent among the persons in this archive — first sanction applied June 2024, making the EU the sole jurisdiction to have sanctioned her so far. Additional designations by the UK and US are possible as documentation of wartime censorship activity accumulates. Interp.
