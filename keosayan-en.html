Tigran Keosayan

Documentary filmmaker for RT
Born: December 3, 1966 — Moscow
Born

Biography
1966–2000
Film industry
Born into a film family — son of director Edmond Keosayan. Studies at VGIK (Moscow film school). Directs feature films and television projects in the post-Soviet period.
2000–2013
Mainstream director
Works across genres — comedy, drama, commercial. Builds a reputation as a technically accomplished director with broad commercial appeal.
2014
Pivot to propaganda
Following the Maidan revolution, begins producing pro-Kremlin documentary content for RT and state television. His films frame Western influence in Ukraine as an existential threat to Russia.
2022
RT films and sanctions
Produces war-justification documentaries for RT during the full-scale invasion. Sanctioned by EU, USA and UK. Wife Simonyan sanctioned by the same three jurisdictions.
2024–2025
The RT tandem
Continues producing documentary content for RT. The Simonyan-Keosayan household functions as the command center of Russia's international information operations.
Documented Quotes

2022
The Method
Professional film techniques deployed as propaganda weapons. Son of Soviet director Edmond Keosayan. Married to Margarita Simonyan. His documentaries use cinematic production values to give Kremlin narratives credibility with educated audiences.
