Yuri
Kovalchuk

The invisible architect of Russia's media landscape.

July 25, 1951, Leningrad
Shareholder, National Media Group; co-owner, Rossiya Segodnya
Channel One, REN TV, Channel Five, CTC, TV3, Peretz
Member of Ozero Cooperative — Putin's inner circle
EU, USA, UK, Canada, Australia
Media oligarch · EU/US Sanctions 2022
01

Biography

Kovalchuk is the man rarely seen on screen, but without whom the screens would not work as they do. Russia's chief media oligarch operates through ownership, not appearances.

1951

Born in Leningrad

Born into an educated family. Graduated from Leningrad State University physics department. Met Putin in the late 1980s — both were members of the Ozero dacha cooperative.

1990s

Bank Rossiya

Co-founded Bank Rossiya in St. Petersburg. The bank rapidly became a financial center for Putin's circle. Kovalchuk is its largest shareholder.

2008

National Media Group

Creates and heads National Media Group — a holding company accumulating stakes in Channel One, REN TV, Channel Five. Quietly but systematically takes control of Russia's largest federal TV networks.

2014

Sanctions after Crimea

The USA imposes personal sanctions on Kovalchuk — among the first businessmen from Putin's circle to be designated. Reason: financing destabilizing activities. Europe follows later. Fact

2022

War and media control

With the full-scale invasion, Kovalchuk's media empire shifts to total war propaganda mode. Not one of his channels broadcast any criticism of the war. EU, UK, and Canada impose personal sanctions. Fact Interp.

02

Quotes

"Russia is living through a historic moment. We are restoring justice."
2022 — interview
"Western media is a weapon. We are building our own weapon."

Cited by Russian media analysts
03

Method

Kovalchuk operates invisibly. He does not host talk shows or give on-camera interviews — he owns those who do. His method: systematic acquisition of media assets, appointment of loyal editors, funding loyalist content. The media empire creates an illusion of diversity — different channels, different formats — with a single editorial line. Interp.

04

Sanctions

Under US sanctions since 2014 — for financially facilitating destabilizing activities. Fact EU imposed personal sanctions in 2022. Fact UK, Canada and Australia followed. Accounts at Bank Rossiya frozen in Western jurisdictions.
