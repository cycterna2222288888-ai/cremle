Sergei
Lavrov

Diplomacy as a weapon. Twenty years of deception.

March 21, 1950, Moscow
Minister of Foreign Affairs (since 2004)
Russia's UN Permanent Representative (1994–2004)
EU, USA, UK, Canada, Australia
MGIMO, 1972 — International Relations
MFA · EU/UK/US Sanctions 2022
01

Biography

Lavrov is Russia's longest-serving post-Soviet foreign minister. A brilliant diplomat of Soviet vintage, he transformed international negotiations into an instrument for buying time and masking aggression.

1950

Born in Moscow

Born in Moscow. Graduated MGIMO in 1972. Polyglot: fluent in English, French, Sinhalese. Begins career at the Soviet MFA immediately.

1972–1994

Soviet and Russian MFA

Serves in embassies in Sri Lanka and the USA, and in MFA headquarters. Rises from attaché to Director of the International Organizations Department. Known for steely composure and sharp tongue.

1994–2004

UN Permanent Representative

Ten years representing Russia on the Security Council. Masters the use of veto, blocking resolutions on Chechnya, Georgia, Ukraine. Builds reputation as an unyielding negotiator.

2004

Foreign Minister

Appointed by Putin. Immediately shifts the tone of Russian diplomacy: from post-Soviet 'adaptation' to a narrative of 'sovereign sphere of influence' and confrontation with the West.

2014

Crimea and denial diplomacy

Publicly denies the presence of Russian troops in Crimea, calling them "local self-defense." Negotiates in Geneva while simultaneously coordinating the occupation. First EU sanctions imposed. Fact

2022

War and isolation

Continues representing Russia at international forums despite full-scale invasion. Uses the UN podium for anti-Ukrainian propaganda. Personal sanctions from EU, USA, UK, Canada, Australia. Fact Interp.

03

Method

Lavrov is a master of 'denial diplomacy.' He uses the language of international law and multipolar rhetoric as a shield to cover aggression. Every time Russia violated international norms, Lavrov stood at the podium with a folder of documents explaining why it was always someone else's fault. His main tool is not blunt lying, but redefinition of reality: an 'invasion' becomes an 'operation,' 'occupation' becomes 'reunification,' 'war crimes' become 'VSU provocations.' Interp.

04

Sanctions

EU personal sanctions since 2022 — for a central role in diplomatic cover for the invasion. Fact UK, USA, Canada and Australia imposed equivalent measures. Entry to EU and UK banned, assets frozen.
