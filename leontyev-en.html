Mikhail Leontyev

"Odnako" (1999–2013), Rosneft press secretary since 2013
Born: October 6, 1958 — Moscow

Biography
1958–1991
Soviet journalism
Born in Moscow. Studies at Moscow State University. Works as a journalist in the Soviet period with a focus on economics and political analysis.
1992–1998
"Sevodnya" and liberal phase
Works at the liberal newspaper "Sevodnya" (Segodnya). Considered a market-liberal commentator in the early post-Soviet years before his pivot.
1999
"Odnako" launches
Begins hosting "Odnako" — a daily 3-minute editorial commentary on Channel One. Over 5,000 episodes over 14 years, making it one of the longest-running political commentary formats in Russian television history.
2013
Rosneft appointment
Leaves Channel One to become press secretary of Rosneft, the state oil company headed by Igor Sechin. The transition illustrates the revolving door between state media and state corporations.
2022–2025
Sanctions and retirement
Sanctioned by EU and UK. Continues in his role at Rosneft. His prolific commentary archive remains a reference document for the evolution of Kremlin messaging from 1999 to the present.

The Method
Intellectual commentary, not shouting. 5,000+ episodes of "Odnako" — the definitive merger of media and the corporate state. Leontyev demonstrates how state propaganda and state corporations are the same institution.
