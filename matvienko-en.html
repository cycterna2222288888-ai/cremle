Valentina
Matvienko

Born in Ukraine. Votes for its destruction.

April 7, 1949, Shepetivka, Ukrainian SSR
Speaker of the Federation Council (since 2011)
Governor of St. Petersburg (2003–2011)
EU, USA, UK, Canada, Australia
United Russia
Federation Council · EU/US/UK Sanctions 2022
01

Biography

Valentina Matvienko — Speaker of the Federation Council, the chamber that on February 22, 2022 authorised the use of Russian armed forces abroad, and in October 2022 ratified the annexation of four Ukrainian regions. She was born in Shepetivka, Ukraine — the very country she voted to invade.

1949

Born in Shepetivka, Ukraine

April 7, 1949, Shepetivka, Ukrainian SSR. Graduated from the Leningrad Chemical-Pharmaceutical Institute. Joined the Communist Party and Komsomol leadership — the standard path to a Soviet political career. Fact

1970s–1990s

Komsomol and diplomatic career

Works in the Komsomol apparatus in Leningrad. Transitions to the Foreign Ministry: ambassador to Malta (1991–1994), then to Greece (1997–1998). Develops connections across party-state and diplomatic networks that will define her later career.

1998–2003

Deputy Prime Minister

Deputy Prime Minister for social affairs under both Yeltsin and Putin. Oversees the social policy block — pensions, education, healthcare. Builds a reputation as an effective implementer rather than an ideologist. Fact

2003–2011

Governor of St. Petersburg

Appointed Governor of St. Petersburg by Putin. Serves two terms. Manages the city through the 2008 financial crisis. Part of the "St. Petersburg group" — the Kremlin elite drawn from Putin's home city and KGB years.

2011–present

Speaker of the Federation Council

Becomes Speaker — chairs the upper house of parliament, which has the constitutional power to authorise the use of Russian armed forces abroad. Fact

22 Feb 2022

Authorisation of the invasion

Chairs the extraordinary Federation Council session authorising the use of armed forces "in the territory of Ukraine" — the formal legal act enabling the full-scale invasion on February 24. Fact

Oct 2022

Annexation ratification

Chairs the Federation Council session ratifying the "accession" of Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia, and Kherson oblasts — territories partially occupied, never fully controlled by Russia, annexed in violation of international law. Sanctioned by EU, USA, UK, Canada, and Australia. Fact

03

Method

Matvienko provides the institutional framework: the Federation Council gives constitutional cover to presidential decisions on war and annexation. Without her chamber's vote on February 22, 2022, Putin would have lacked the formal legal basis for the invasion. She presides over a body that rubber-stamps, but the rubber stamp matters — it allows the Kremlin to claim the war was authorised by parliament, not ordered by one man. Interp.

Matvienko's biographical paradox — born in Ukraine, now presiding over the legislative act that destroyed Ukrainian statehood — is part of her value to the system. She embodies the claim that the war is not about ethnicity but about "geopolitics." Her Ukrainian origin allows her to appear in state media denying the "Russophobia" narrative while simultaneously ratifying the annexation of Ukrainian territory. Interp.

As Speaker she controls the Federation Council's agenda, committee appointments, and international delegations. She is the face of Russian parliamentary diplomacy — providing legitimising contact for foreign politicians while the war continues, allowing them to claim "engagement with Russia" without direct Kremlin meetings. Interp.

04

Sanctions

EU — personal sanctions since 2022 (Council Regulation EU 2022/330). Ground: "responsible for approving the use of Russian armed forces in Ukraine" and ratification of annexation. Asset freeze, travel ban. Fact

USA — OFAC designation (Executive Order 14024). Full asset freeze. Fact

UK — Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019. Asset freeze and travel ban. Fact

Canada — Special Economic Measures (Russia) Regulations. Fact

Australia — Autonomous Sanctions (Designated Persons and Entities — Russia) Instrument. Fact
