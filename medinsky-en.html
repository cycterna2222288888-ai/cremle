Vladimir Medinsky

Presidential adviser, ex-Minister of Culture 2012–2020, chief "peace" negotiator
Born: July 18, 1970 — Smela, Ukraine
Born

Biography
1970–1998
Kyiv origins
Born in Smela, Ukrainian SSR. Grows up in Moscow. Studies at MGIMO (Moscow State Institute of International Relations). Later completes a doctorate in history — subsequently found by academic councils to contain systematic fabrications.
1998–2012
Political career
Works in public relations and enters politics through United Russia. Becomes a Duma deputy. Develops a public profile as an author of nationalist historical revisionism ("Myths About Russia" series).
2012
Minister of Culture
Appointed Minister of Culture by Putin. Over eight years, transforms cultural policy into an instrument of state ideology: film subsidies tied to patriotic narratives, monuments to Soviet figures, suppression of independent cultural institutions.
2022
Lead negotiator
Heads the Russian delegation at the Belarus and Istanbul "peace" talks in February–March 2022. The terms Russia presents — Ukrainian disarmament, NATO ban, regime change — are publicly stated to be non-negotiable. The talks collapse. Sanctioned by EU, USA, UK and Canada.
2024–2025
Trump-era negotiations
Returns to the negotiating table in the context of Trump's pressure for a settlement. Continues to represent positions incompatible with Ukrainian sovereignty.

The Method
Rewriting history as a state project. Culture as a weapon. His doctoral dissertation was found to contain fabrications — yet he led Russia's cultural policy for eight years. Born in Ukraine. Led the 2022 "peace" talks with terms designed to be rejected.
