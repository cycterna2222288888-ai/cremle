Elena
Mizulina

The Architect of Censorship. Laws as weapons.

January 9, 1954, Bui, Kostroma Oblast
Senator, Federation Council (since 2013)
State Duma Deputy (1999–2013)
EU (2022), UK (2022)
Nikolai Mizulin — head of the Safe Internet League
Senator · EU Sanctions 2022
01

Biography

Elena Mizulina is the chief architect of repressive legislation in post-2000 Russia. Her name is associated with laws that criminalized dissent, LGBT identity, criticism of the military, and 'disrespect' for the state.

1954

Birth

Born in Kostroma Oblast. Graduated from the Law Faculty of Yaroslavl State University. PhD in criminal law. Taught law in the 1980–90s.

1999

Entry into politics

Elected to the State Duma from Yabloko, later joins A Just Russia. Chairs the Committee on Family, Women and Children — a tool she uses to advance a conservative agenda.

2013

LGBT propaganda law

Personally drafts and shepherds through the Duma the "gay propaganda law." The law bans "propaganda of non-traditional sexual relationships" to minors. Draws international condemnation. Fact

2013

Transfer to Federation Council

Becomes a senator from Omsk Oblast. Continues legislative work in the upper chamber while maintaining media visibility.

2022

War and censorship

Actively supports the invasion. Her son Nikolai Mizulin heads the Safe Internet League — the primary tool for blocking independent media and messengers. Mizulina herself publicly demands the 'shutdown' of opposition resources. EU and UK impose sanctions. Fact Interp.

02

03

Method

Mizulina works through legislation. She does not agitate on TV screens — she writes repression into the legal system. The 2013 'gay propaganda' law created a precedent: it was followed by laws on 'discrediting the army,' 'fakes about the war,' and 'disrespecting state symbols.' Each law expanded the space for criminal prosecution of dissent. Her son runs the internet censorship tool — a family business built on repression. Interp.

04

Sanctions

EU personal sanctions since 2022 — for supporting the invasion and providing legislative tools for political repression. Fact UK imposed equivalent sanctions. Entry banned, assets frozen.
