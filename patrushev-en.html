Nikolai
Patrushev

The grey cardinal. Architect of the security state.

July 11, 1951, Leningrad
Presidential Adviser (since 2023)
Security Council Secretary (2008–2023), FSB Director (1999–2008)
EU, USA, UK
One of Putin's closest allies since the 1990s
Silovik · EU/UK/US Sanctions 2022
01

Biography

Nikolai Patrushev — the KGB veteran who built the FSB into an instrument of political power, then spent 15 years as Security Council Secretary shaping Russia's most aggressive foreign policy decisions, including the decision to launch the full-scale invasion of Ukraine.

1951

Born in Leningrad

July 11, 1951, Leningrad (now St. Petersburg). Graduated from the Leningrad Shipbuilding Institute. Recruited by the KGB while still a student — the standard path for technical specialists of his generation. Fact

1970s–1990s

KGB career

Works in KGB counter-intelligence in Leningrad. Meets Putin — they share the same Leningrad KGB background. After the Soviet collapse moves to the FSK (FSB predecessor), then to the FSB central apparatus in Moscow. Builds a network of personal loyalty within the service.

1999–2008

FSB Director

Appointed FSB Director when Putin became Prime Minister and then President. Under his leadership the FSB expands into counter-extremism, economic surveillance, and political control. His nine-year tenure makes him the second-longest-serving FSB director. Fact

2008–2022

Security Council Secretary

Becomes Secretary of the Security Council — formally advisory, in practice the ideological core of Putin's inner circle. Participates in decisions on the Georgia war (2008), Crimea annexation (2014), Donbas support, and Syria. Regarded as Putin's closest ideological ally among the siloviki. Fact

2021

The article on Ukraine

Publishes an article in "Rossiyskaya Gazeta" arguing that Ukrainians and Russians are "one people," that Ukraine as a state is an "artificial creation," and that Ukrainian statehood should be dissolved. The article is the ideological companion to Putin's July 2021 essay and a blueprint for 2022 invasion narratives. Interp.

2022

Full-scale war

One of the key architects of the invasion decision. After the invasion publicly states: "Ukraine as a state has no future." EU, US, and UK sanctions imposed. Fact

2023–2025

Presidential Adviser

Formally moved from Security Council Secretary to Presidential Adviser — a possible sign of marginalization after the Prigozhin mutiny, or a lateral shift maintaining informal influence. Continues to appear at Kremlin meetings and give ideological interviews. Interp.

03

Method

Patrushev is an ideologist, not a propagandist. He does not appear on television every day — he publishes articles in official newspapers, gives interviews to state agencies, and delivers speeches at Security Council meetings. His function is to provide the philosophical and historical justification for Kremlin policy, including the denial of Ukrainian statehood and the framing of the war as civilisational self-defence. Interp.

His 2021 article in "Rossiyskaya Gazeta" directly anticipated Putin's essay of the same year and became part of the official ideological canon: Ukrainians do not exist as a separate people, their state is an "artificial project" of the West, and its "denazification" is historically necessary. These theses provided the narrative framework that state media used to justify the invasion to Russian audiences in February–March 2022. Interp.

Patrushev's influence is institutional as well as ideological. As Security Council Secretary he controlled intelligence coordination, inter-agency information flows, and the threat assessment process that shaped presidential decisions. His 15-year tenure means that many current senior FSB, SVR, and GRU officers owe their careers to his patronage — giving him structural influence independent of his formal title. Interp.

04

Sanctions

EU — personal sanctions since 2022 (Council Regulation EU 2022/330). Ground: "responsible for actively supporting and implementing actions and policies which undermine the territorial integrity, sovereignty and independence of Ukraine." Asset freeze, travel ban to 27 EU states. Fact

USA — OFAC designation (Executive Order 14024). Full asset freeze in US jurisdiction, prohibition on all transactions. Fact

UK — equivalent measures under the Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019. Asset freeze, travel ban. Fact
