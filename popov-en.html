Evgeny Popov

"60 Minutes" (co-hosted with wife Skabeeva), State Duma Deputy

July 25, 1971, Moscow
EU, USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan
Депутат-пропагандист · ЕС
01

Biography

1971–2000

Early career

Born in Moscow. Journalism graduate. Begins working at state television in the 1990s, rising through the ranks at VGTRK.

2001–2015

State TV anchor

Becomes an anchor on Russia-1. Works as a war correspondent during the 2008 war in Georgia, covering it from the Russian military's perspective.

2016

"60 Minutes" and marriage

Launches "60 Minutes" together with Skabeeva on Russia-1. The show becomes a flagship format: daily two-hour political talk with a scripted anti-Western agenda.

2021

Enters parliament

Elected to the State Duma on the United Russia ticket. Becomes the only person simultaneously serving as a Duma deputy and co-hosting a daily prime-time propaganda show.

2022–2025

Dual mandate

Continues both roles through the full-scale invasion. Sanctioned by six jurisdictions together with his wife. On the show: "The Anglosaxons want to fight Russia to the last Ukrainian."
