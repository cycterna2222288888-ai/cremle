01

Biography

1984–2006

Early career

Born in Volzhsky, Volgograd region. Studies journalism in Moscow. Begins in regional television before moving to federal channels.

2007–2015

State TV correspondent

Works as a correspondent for Russia-1. Covers Crimea in 2014 with a pronounced pro-annexation line. Meets future husband Evgeny Popov on set.

2016

"60 Minutes" launches

Co-hosts "60 Minutes" with Popov on Russia-1. The show quickly becomes one of the most-watched propaganda talk formats: two hosts, a panel of loyalists, and a token "foreign guest" to be shouted down.

2022

Third World War

On live television, Skabeeva declares: "We are openly at war with NATO. Let's call things by their names: the Third World War has begun." Sanctioned by EU, USA, UK, Canada, Australia and Japan.

2024–2025

Permanent war footing

60 Minutes airs every weekday. Skabeeva continues to frame every development — military, diplomatic or political — as a Western conspiracy against Russia. The format has not changed.
