Vladimir Solovyov

Biography
1963–1991
Origins
Born Vladimir Solovyov in Moscow. Studies at the Institute of Steel and Alloys. Works as a journalist in the late Soviet period. Adopts a new surname (birth name: Shapiro) early in his career.
1992–2004
Rise on liberal radio
Works at Radio Maximum and later Echo of Moscow. Co-hosts "We" on NTV. Builds a reputation as a sharp debate moderator — before pivoting entirely toward the state narrative.
2005–2013
"Evening with Solovyov" launches
Flagship talk show on Russia-1 becomes a nightly propaganda fixture. Solovyov perfects his formula: invite critics, then shout them down. Ratings soar.
2014
Ukraine — the turning point
Annexation of Crimea and the Donbas war give Solovyov his defining stage. Night after night he frames the conflict as a Nazi coup backed by Washington. The show transitions from political talk to war propaganda.
2022
Full-scale invasion
On the night of February 24, Solovyov opens his broadcast celebrating the "special military operation." Calls for nuclear strikes. EU, US, UK and five other jurisdictions impose personal sanctions. Italian authorities seize his Lake Como villas and yacht.
2024–2025
Escalation as brand
As the war enters its third year with no end in sight, Solovyov's rhetoric intensifies further. He frames any peace negotiation as betrayal. His program remains in prime-time on state television every weeknight.

The Method
Rally-style aggression, personal attacks, hysterical pace. Creates a siege-fortress atmosphere — "us vs. the entire world." Owns villas on Lake Como worth €8M, seized by Italian authorities under sanctions.
