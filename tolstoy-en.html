Pyotr Tolstoy

Deputy Speaker of the State Duma, former Channel One Deputy Director-General

August 20, 1969, Moscow
EU, USA, UK, Canada
Вице-спикер · Персона нон грата
01

Biography

1969–1994

The Tolstoy name

Born into the Tolstoy family — great-great-grandson of Leo Tolstoy. Studies at Moscow State University. The family name opens doors in both literary and political circles.

1994–2016

Channel One executive

Rises through Channel One to become Deputy Director-General. Hosts political programs. Becomes one of the most recognizable faces of state television's transition to full propaganda mode.

2016

Enters parliament

Elected to the State Duma. Becomes Deputy Speaker, combining parliamentary authority with his media legacy. Advocates for restrictions on NGOs and "foreign agents."

2022

Escalation — and sanctions

Calls for "denazification" of Ukraine and frames the invasion as a civilizational struggle. Sanctioned by EU, USA, UK and Canada. Declared persona non grata in Latvia and Estonia.

2024–2025

Historical revisionism

Promotes the "historical Russian lands" narrative. In 2025, states that any negotiation must begin with Ukrainian recognition of Russia's annexations — framing surrender as precondition for talks.

03

The Method

Aristocratic surname as legitimacy. The great-great-grandson of Leo Tolstoy deploying that name in service of imperial war propaganda. Former Deputy Director-General of Channel One, now Deputy Speaker of the State Duma. Persona non grata in Latvia and Estonia. Interp.
